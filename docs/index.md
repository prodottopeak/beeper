# Beeper

## Stay aware of time, without watching the clock.

Beeper gives you gentle recurring chimes throughout the day, helping you stay aware of time without interrupting what you're doing.

Whether you're working, studying, meditating, building routines, or simply trying to stay present, Beeper gives you subtle reminders that fit quietly into your day.

## Features

- Recurring timers and interval chimes
- Gentle sounds, bells, and tones
- Custom active hours and schedules
- Snap timers to the clock
- Simple, distraction-free design
- No account required
- No ads

## Beeper Premium

Unlock more with Premium:

- Multiple schedules
- Premium sound packs
- Sound rotation
- Quick setups for routines

## Designed to stay out of your way

Beeper isn't another productivity system to manage.

Set your rhythm, put your phone away, and let gentle cues keep you aware of time.
