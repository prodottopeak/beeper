Do I need to keep Beeper open?

With notifications allowed, Beeper can send recurring cues in the background. Delivery is managed by iOS. Optional “Play in silent mode” needs separate alarm permission; alarms require dismissal and unattended delivery is subject to system limits.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. Premium is a one-time purchase. The App Store shows the price for your region before you buy.

Can I write my own reminder?

Yes. Custom reminder messages are available with intervals of at least five minutes. A message explains what the next cue is for.
